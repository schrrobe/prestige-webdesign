## Surface

**Mode:** Persuade. The whole website, with the homepage leading. Every subpage inherits the world.

**Audience:** owners of small and medium-sized businesses in the Ruhr area, often on a phone.

**Action:** request a free first consultation. On the homepage this happens through the short form under the lead: topic plus email, message optional.

**Proof:** three live references (Shape & Flow as the lead story), "ab" prices, the process, and confirmed figures.

**Constraints:**
- URLs, JSON-LD, sitemap and the noindex split stay unchanged.
- WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Ich-Form.
- No photo.
- No invented proof.
- No theme or metaphor (user requirement).

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Like a well-made magazine: high-contrast serif headlines, plenty of air, strong pictures, sober text. It rejects the agency template (dark hero, glass, icon-tile rows) and every themed costume (stadium, building plan, Zeche).

**OWN-WORLD:**
- Cool white (#fbfbfa) on ink (#161616), with stone (#ece9e4) for alternating sections.
- Oxblood (#7a1f2b) is the only colour: actions, and at most one italic accent word per view.
- Bodoni Moda (opsz, with italic) for display. Schibsted Grotesk for text and UI.
- 1px hairlines, an asymmetric 12-column grid, captions below images.
- Pill buttons with an arrow.
- Underlined form fields.
- Footer on ink, final CTA on oxblood.
- Light by default, with a dark mode.

**STORY:** within 10 seconds the visitor understands: web design from Dortmund, carefully made, fixed price from 800 €, one contact person. They believe it through the large Shape & Flow lead image and open prices. They act: topic, email, "Erstgespräch anfragen".

**FIRST VIEWPORT:**
- Left, 7 columns: a large serif H1 "Webdesign aus Dortmund, *mit Sorgfalt* gemacht." with the italic word in oxblood. Below it a lead naming the Ruhr area and "Festpreis ab 800 €", then the inline short form (topic chips, email field and pill button in one row), then a fact line in small grotesk (since 2020 · 10+ projects · reply within 24 h · 100 % satisfaction).
- Right, offset: the Shape & Flow screenshot as the lead image with the mobile view overlapping, and a caption below.
- Mobile: H1, lead, form, fact line, image.

**FORM:** Editorial (round 4, safer register, chosen by the user from three conventional directions). Seed key 5ef02ebe. Signature: the lead image with caption and an overlapping mobile view. The italic accent word is the only colour in the headline. Motion: a single soft reveal of the lead image (clip-path from the bottom), plus an arrow nudge on buttons.
